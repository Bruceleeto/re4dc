RE4 Dreamcast - edge smoothing (FSAA) test disc, 2026-10-14
Private test, not a release. Test 10 plus one change: the Rendering row is back in Options.

Disc: gdemu\ folder (disc.gdi, track01.bin, track02.raw, track03.bin). Checksums are in SHA256SUMS.txt.

HOW TO SWITCH
Options (title screen or pause menu) > RENDERING: QUALITY / FSAA / FSAA LITE.
Left/right changes it at once. The level is NOT saved: every boot starts in QUALITY.
The level name shows at the top of the screen for 3 seconds after a switch.
Please don't press X + START (the old look toggle) while testing. It can undo FSAA LITE's setting.

THE THREE LEVELS
QUALITY    The test 10 picture. Use it as the reference.
FSAA       Renders twice as wide and halves it, with the normal TV flicker filter left on.
           Edges should be smoother with no more flicker than QUALITY.
           Costs twice the pixel work, so it may be slower outdoors (fog, mist).
FSAA LITE  Renders twice as wide but half as tall, then the Dreamcast scales it back up to full height.
           Same pixel work as QUALITY. Nobody knows yet how the real hardware does the 2x height
           scale. It may look smooth, it may flicker like the old trial, or the picture may be wrong.
           That is what this test is for.

The old trial disc (aa-trial, "640x240 FSAA") flickered because it showed 240 lines, one on each
TV field. Each field starts half a line lower, so every horizontal edge moved up and down
30 times a second. Both new levels send a full 480-line picture, the same way QUALITY does.

WHERE TO LOOK (same spot, switch levels, compare)
1. Thin horizontal edges: roof edges, window frames, door tops, the wooden beams in the
   village square (r101). Do they flicker or twitch compared with QUALITY?
2. Fences and thin poles: the fence along the path to the village and the poles in the square.
   Are the stair-step edges smoother? Do they crawl when the camera turns?
3. Menus and text: Options, the inventory, subtitles. Does the text shimmer?
4. Leon's outline and hair against the sky.
5. Speed: R + START cycles the speed display (VMU / screen). Note the fps in the square
   with villagers around, in the house fight, and outdoors in mist, for each level.

PLEASE REPORT
For FSAA and FSAA LITE: flicker (less / same / more than QUALITY), edge smoothness,
anything broken (black or squashed picture, stripes, a garbage line at the bottom), and the
fps compared with QUALITY. A phone video of a still scene after you switch levels helps a lot.
If FSAA LITE looks broken, switch back to QUALITY. That should restore the picture.
Switching levels has crashed once before (test 8, issue #26), so please note any crash a few
seconds after a switch.
