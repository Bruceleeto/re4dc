## Skipped side effects and why they are bounded

Do not claim that the old function is entirely side-effect-free.

1. `invis_pco_why` counters and lazy-build log/counter timing can differ. These are diagnostics, not source state, lifecycle or presentation authority. Preserve the caller's normal `invis_pco_tests` accounting unless its meaning is deliberately documented; do not fabricate the old reason counts or add production per-actor logging.
2. `gate_init` may lazily populate the fixed cast pregate ball arrays. It reads immutable cast positions/weights/bind matrices and writes its private `gate_*` arrays and counters. It does not write source matrices, actor state, leases or owner generations. For ordinary four-cast-info actors, the preceding `invis_owner` has already called it when it reaches this work. For original-hand/fifth-info cases, this early return may defer a cast appearance's first warming. No new pool, allocation or lifetime is introduced.
3. `invis_mesh_for` may populate/replace its fixed 48-entry/1536-ball source-bound cache, advance its private replacement cursors or remember failure. It reads source ModelData and bind matrices; its only mutation is derived render-bound cache state and diagnostics. It is also used by the unchanged source visibility path. Skipping a warm-up can change future cache contents, build timing, replacement order and conservative-cull availability. **Cache-byte identity and identical future TA submission counts are not established by the predicate proof.**

The cache consumers already require a usable bound; failure retains rendering rather than establishing a new cull. An absent warm-up must still build the same source-derived bound on demand or take that existing no-cull fallback. Thus omitting optional warming has no authority to alter source gameplay/pose or ownership/lifecycle, and must not remove visible content. The required tests below explicitly cover deferred warming, cache replacement/failure and active-cell transitions. If an implementation relies on warming to maintain a hidden invariant or a cache miss changes visible/native lifecycle behavior, stop this candidate; do not silently invent new invalidation, caching or fallback behavior. No cache keys, capacity, invalidation, failure meaning or consumer policy may change in this trial.
