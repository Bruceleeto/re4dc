# Character shadows on the Dreamcast (shadow lane, 2026-10-14)

Policy (user): Dreamcast-native rendering instead of GameCube techniques. The PVR's cheap-shadow modifier volumes
give hardware shadows. Simplest robust scheme first; fancier variants are costed options only. One light source for
character light and shadows: the per-actor light probe of the lighting lane (`actor_light_probe.h`, lane/light-p2).

Labels: **modelled** = SH-4 hardware model (hwproject.sh) or the PVR estimate below; **Flycast count** = an
emulator counter (TA_BIN_DIAG, MV stats lines). No console measurements yet.

## 1. What the port draws today (test 10 recipe)

| Shadow | GC | PS2 | Test 10 port |
|---|---|---|---|
| Projected character shadows (`ShadowTrans`, `ShadowMng`: the model rendered from a type-4 shadow light into an I8 texture, projected, soft passes through EFB copies) | yes, rooms with shadow lights | no export of `ShadowTrans` / `ShadowMng` in the SLUS-211.34 export table (only `DrawFootShadow`, `cPlayer::shadowCtrl`, `cSubChar::shadowCtrl`): dropped as far as the table shows | not drawn; 0 instructions in the r101 square and r100 H-fight hw-model windows |
| Foot shadows (`DrawFootShadow`: a shadow sprite per joint of the character's foot shadow table, projected along each mode-5 type-4 light onto the floor, alpha by distance) | yes | yes (exported) | cut: `FX_LEAN=1` (safecuts.mk S5) returns at the top |
| Static room shadows | SHD objects + lit vertices | baked into the PS2 world vertex colours / vertex alpha | PS2 vertex colours; `PS2_VERTEX_ALPHA=1` draws the soft ground shadow overlays (#20) |

So test 10 draws **no character shadows at all**. `MV_SHADOWS` (440416c3, cheap-shadow prisms) and `SHADOW_BLOB`
(b6fac130, PS2-style TR blobs) were already in the tree, default 0, never in a recipe, and never costed.

PVR setup already in the tree (MV_SHADOWS=1): OP_MOD object pointer blocks of 8 words (48,128 B per bank, paid by the
vertex buffer, texture pool unchanged), cheap-shadow scale register set at init (`pvr_set_shadow_scale`), pvr_recover
chains the OP_MOD list-done event, TA_BIN_DIAG models the volume list (`tabin mod:`). No TR_MOD list, no
two-parameter receivers.

## 2. Options (worst views with enemies)

Windows: r101 square fight `perf-r101sq` global ticks 1330:1409; r100 outdoor H-fight `r100-h-fight` 2300:2379.
CPU = hw model of `re4dc_mv_emit` (volume build, clip, cap, projection, inlined), per traced frame.
GPU = modelled ISP: every volume triangle costs 40 cycles (32 tile rows + ~8 setup) in each tile its bounding box
touches (the TA_BIN_DIAG entry count, an upper bound), 100 MHz. TA = bytes of volume list per scene.

| Option | Look | CPU (modelled) | PVR (modelled from Flycast counts) | Memory |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| (a) keep test 10 | no character shadows | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| (b) **MV_SHADOWS, round prism per actor, straight down** | hard-edged dark octagon under each character, 50 % | r101 0.06 ms (drawn frames) to 0.12 ms (all traced); r100 0.16 to 0.20 ms | volume tris/frame mean 73-77, max 112-140; tile entries mean 215-239, max 450 -> ISP mean 0.09-0.10 ms, max 0.18 ms; TA ~1.9 KB per caster (4.7 KB per scene mean in the H-fight, 23 KB at 12 casters) | 96 KB VRAM (2 banks, from the vertex buffer); overflow pool peak 92.6 KB of 230.4 KB |
| (b') + light probe stretch (Leon, ACTOR_PROBE_LIGHT=1) | Leon's footprint stretched away from his dominant light by up to 1.8 m (mean 1.0 m in the H-fight) | fixed-light worst case (every caster stretched, MV_DIR_AZ/EL): r101 0.20 ms, r100 0.23-0.28 ms; probe-only-Leon is between (b) and this | larger footprints: more tile entries, same triangle count | same |
| (c) SHADOW_BLOB, one soft TR blob per actor | soft round blob, alpha 120; barely visible on the r100 leaf ground in Flycast | not measured on the hw model (one strip fan per caster, no floor query) | +6k to +13k translucent pixels per frame (TSP fill, ~0.06-0.13 ms at 1 px/clock) | none |
| (d) restore the game's own `DrawFootShadow` (FX_LEAN foot part off) | PS2/GC look, needs the room's mode-5 type-4 lights | not measured (floor query + GX sprites per joint, the reason it was cut) | TR sprites | none |
| (e) MV + TR_MOD list (shadows also on PS2 vertex-alpha ground layers) | shadow survives under translucent ground overlays (r106, r104, r10x) | +~same CPU (second send) | +same ISP again, second OPB set | +96 KB VRAM |
| (f) two-parameter (normal) modifier volumes, per-actor strength / soft rim | per-actor intensity from the probe | receivers need two colour/UV sets | world vertex share doubles (TA bank peak already 1.67 of 2 MB) | not affordable on the whole world |

Cheap shadow mode has one global intensity register, so per-actor strength (the probe's suggested strength) is not
possible without (f). Volumes are inclusion-only and cannot be soft.

Whole-frame impact: (b) is under 0.3 ms of a 60-69 ms modelled r101 / r100 fight frame on the CPU and ~0.1-0.2 ms of
PVR time; the hw-model frame totals of the knob-off and MV arms are within run-to-run noise (60.63 vs 60.74 ms
drawn r101; 68.81 vs 68.71 ms r100).

## 3. Recommendation and what changed (89fd11a1, lane/shadow on lane/t11 af0eedf6)

Ship (b)+(b'): `MV_SHADOWS=1` (alias `SHADOW_MV=1`) with `ACTOR_PROBE_LIGHT=1` (lighting lane's choice for test 12):
Leon's footprint follows his probe; Ganados (no probe at =1) keep the round straight-down footprint. No new kernel,
no new list; the receivers, OPB and recovery code were already in the tree.

`mv_shadow.cpp` change: per caster, `re4dc_actor_light_probe(model)` (only when the build defines
`RE4DC_ACTOR_LIGHT_PROBE_API`); world direction = -(V^T dir); stretch = MV_BODY (1700 mm) x horizontal/vertical
slope x coherence, cap MV_LEN_MAX (1800 mm). Same triangle count (the hull of the feet circle and its shifted copy is
still convex, so the near-plane clip and cap are unchanged). `MV_DIR_AZ` / `MV_DIR_EL`: one fixed light for test
builds.

## 4. Gates

- Knob off: play and warp images byte-identical to test 10 (`t10stP` image + 6 overlays, elfdiff).
- H2 logic trace, MV_SHADOWS=1 vs padded control: STRICT 0..4701, decision_cmp MUST-IDENTICAL, s30 340/340
  (heap_before 170,272 both), overruns 0.
- MV + ACTOR_PROBE_LIGHT=1 + LEON_SOURCE_LIGHT=1 (local merge of lane/light-p2) vs the same without MV, padded:
  STRICT 0..4701, MUST-IDENTICAL, s30 340/340 (heap_before 166,176 both: the probe build's own heap 4 cost,
  -4,096 against test 10), overruns 0.
- TEX_CHECK=2 + MCHECK_PT + MCHECK_LEONGC with MV_SHADOWS=1: h2 (r100), square (r101) and r106 entry PASS.
- r106 entry / path views: no caster on screen (Leon's prism below the frame, the hall Ganados out of view), so no
  volume is sent there (TA_BIN_DIAG: 0 volume tile entries).
- Flycast renderers: the default DX11 and the per-pixel DX11 OIT (`pvr.rend = 6`) give the same footprint (3,307 vs
  3,315 changed pixels, same box). Only opaque receivers carry the bit and only the OP_MOD list is sent, which on
  the PVR affects opaque and punch-through polygons, so both renderers match the hardware rule here; the renderers
  differ only for translucent receivers (option (e)).

## 5. Traps

- Receivers are MeshDraw parts in the opaque list only. PS2_VERTEX_ALPHA ground layers (translucent) are not
  shadowed and draw over the shadowed opaque ground: option (e).
- The RE4 over-the-shoulder camera hides Leon's feet most of the time; his shadow shows mainly when walking with the
  light behind him. Ganados at mid range show theirs.
- PACE_MODE=fast arms are not frame-comparable across render knobs (drop patterns differ): look stills use
  PACE_MODE=off arms frozen at a warp `freeze` tick.
- Rebased onto lane/t11 (af0eedf6, T11REC has ACTOR_PROBE_LIGHT=1 LEON_SOURCE_LIGHT=1 LEON_LIGHT_HEADROOM=1): the T11REC
  warp arm + MV_SHADOWS=1 builds clean (missing.txt empty, mv_shadow.o calls re4dc_actor_light_probe). The trace and
  check gates above ran on the a65045fe base plus a local lane/light-p2 merge; knob-off identity on lane/t11 holds
  by construction (the change is inside the MV_SHADOWS block of game30.mk and mv_shadow.cpp, built only with it).
